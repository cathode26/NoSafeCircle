NSC-003 manual Play Mode revalidation — 2026-08-24

Before the repair, clicking diagonally through a wall could leave the wizard attempting the
unreachable destination indefinitely. The character appeared nearly still but continued
making tiny horizontal progress, which prevented door opening because interaction kept
observing movement.

After the NSC-003 obstruction repair, the same behavior is materially improved and accepted
as good enough for the current prototype: the wizard may slide briefly against collision,
then the obstructed destination is cancelled and the character becomes genuinely stationary
instead of creeping indefinitely.

Manual disposition: approved for the current prototype and NSC-003 revalidation.
