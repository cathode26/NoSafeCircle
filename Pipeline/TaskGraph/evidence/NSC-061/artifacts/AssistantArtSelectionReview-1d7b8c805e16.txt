== Clause 1: equally intentional representation across all four combinations (measured) ==

Representation equality is measured, not judged. Within the PixelLab/ (180px) tree that this
task authorizes (see Clause 2), every one of the four combinations -- feminine-dark,
feminine-light, masculine-dark, masculine-light -- has an identical structure: the same two
poses (standing, walk), the same eight PixelLab base directions in each pose, and the same
56 frames per combination (8 standing + 8 directions x 6 walk frames). This is deterministic
and was re-verified in this session (see VAL-001 artifact): all four combos, both trees,
8/8 directions, 4/4 diagonal walk facings, identical frame counts. No combination has fewer
frames, a missing direction, or a lower-resolution/lower-frame-count substitute than any
other. This structural symmetry is what "equally intentional representation" means for this
gate and it is fully deterministic; it says nothing about subjective art quality, which is
addressed narrowly in Clause 3 below.

== Clause 3: dark-but-cute horror-comedy direction (assessed, with an explicit limit) ==

NOT independently visually re-inspected pixel-by-pixel in this session: the assistant did not
open and compare all 224 authorized frames (or the further 224 in PixelLab128, which is out
of this gate's scope per Clause 2) against the art bible by eye, and makes no pixel-level
art-direction judgment of its own here. This gate's assessment rests on two things only:
  (1) this is the same family PIXELLAB_GENERATION.md already selected and documented against
      AC-002/AC-003 -- shared low top-down camera, proportions, clothing, palette, and the
      "dark-but-cute horror-comedy" prompt language quoted in that file, applied identically
      across all four option-specific opening traits (masculine-dark, feminine-dark,
      masculine-light, feminine-light) with none preferred or downgraded; and
  (2) the deterministic structural completeness re-verified in this session under VAL-001 and
      Clause 1 above (equal frame counts, no missing direction, no lower-resolution
      substitute for any one combination).

No claim is made here that goes beyond those two things. In particular: no claim that any
frame is free of a visual defect, no re-diff of any individual frame's pixels (unlike the
NSC-063 precedent's pixel-diff re-verification, which does not apply here since NSC-061 has
no known reported visual regression to re-check), and no claim about the PixelLab128 tree's
art direction, which belongs to NSC-095's own VAL-002.
