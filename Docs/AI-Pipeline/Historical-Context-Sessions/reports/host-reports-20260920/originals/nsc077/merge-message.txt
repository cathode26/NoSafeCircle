Merge NSC-077: enemy art integration for the existing moving enemies

The Dungeon Brute (MeleeEnemy) and the Lantern Wraith now use their approved PixelLab art in-game (contract revision 10).
- EnemyAnimationAssetBuilder imports the 16 NSC-063 idle stills and 96 NSC-093 walk frames in place:
  - Sprite, Texture2D shape, cookie defaults reset;
  - 64 pixels per unit, Point filter, no mipmaps, ground-line pivots.
  It generates MeleeEnemyAnimator and LanternWraithAnimator, each with 8-direction idle and walk clips.
- DoorPrototypeGlobalSceneBuilder gives each enemy:
