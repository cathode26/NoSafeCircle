Tests: validate spawn placement by floor underfoot, not a 0.1 sample radius

EnemySpawnPlacementTests rejected the two Final Room guards at (8, 0, 74) and
(-8, 0, 77) - spawns this fix never moved. Probing the committed scene in Unity
6000.1.8f1 showed why: Room_FinalRoom/GameplayGeometry/FloorCollision sits
directly under both, and the nearest navigable point is (8, 0.136, 74) and
(-8, 0.117, 77): zero horizontal displacement, only a vertical offset above the
test's 0.1 radius. The bake's sampled surface rises from 0.083 on that room's
centre line to 0.156 beside its benches, while every other room is flat at
0.083, so 0.1 was smaller than the bake's own vertical error there. The scene
does carry live baked NavMeshData, so the assertion was measuring something
real - just measuring it the wrong way.

The check now states what the design requires, navigable floor underfoot: a 0.5
vertical allowance, which the sample radius enforces, plus a 0.05 horizontal one
in DescribeHorizontalDisplacement. Both halves are proven rather than trusted,
each where it actually lives:

- PlacementCheck_RejectsAProbeStandingInARoomWall, on the real scene, that a
  point on the Final Room's west wall centre line is rejected by the check. The
  bake carves the wall, so what rejects it is the sample radius.
- HorizontalRule_AcceptsTheMeasuredFinalRoomOffset_AndRejectsADisplacedSample,
  on the rule directly, that a sample 0.136 above and exactly underfoot is
  accepted while the same offset displaced 0.4 sideways is not. No authored point
  in this scene sits beside a gap narrow enough to exercise that half through the
  bake, so proving it through the bake is not possible.

The 0.5 allowance is deliberately not larger: the benches are 0.7 tall with
walkable polygons on top at 0.48, so a wider allowance would start admitting
spawns buried in props, leaving only the capsule overlap check to catch them.
