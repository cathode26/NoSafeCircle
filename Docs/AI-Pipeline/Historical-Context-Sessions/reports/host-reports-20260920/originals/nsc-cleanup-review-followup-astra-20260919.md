## Cleanup role and recurrence prevention

The role now owns registration accounting, bounded drift/exception handling, recovery-backed human batches and outcome checks. Producers own creations, descendants, outputs and closeout. Registration is self-service; Cleanup does not approve every new workspace. This is a workable manual starting point and correctly admits that launcher enforcement, automatic reconciliation and a schedule are not installed.

Former silence-as-release, age/empty-restore-log deletion, Logs-as-noise and worktrees-only-risk language has been superseded. Explicit protection for `art-rejects/NSC-078` and `art-rejects/NSC-095` is restored in the guide, startup prompt and queue.

Dynamic fixture names do not need individual preregistration; persistent/retained clones and surviving leftovers do. Logs and unique outputs stay outside fixture teardown, and no new general cleanup authority is granted. This resolves the excess-bureaucracy issue without allowing unregistered persistent scratch repositories.
