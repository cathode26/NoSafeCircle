Clear the pending locked-door hit when the door reports broken

Two pursuing enemies can wind up against the same locked door. Only the enemy
whose hit broke the door cleared its pending door; the other kept the broken
door until its next Tick, which NSC-017 AC-004 and VAL-004 do not allow.
Subscribe to DoorInteractable.Broken while a door is pending, so every attacker
clears in the same frame, and unsubscribe on reset, disable and destroy.
