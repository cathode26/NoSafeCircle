NSC-078: read the generation estimate in meter terms, not printed costs

The NSC-095 wizard run measured the PixelLab account meter moving 130
generations against 93 of printed per-call costs, about 37% more, with no other
PixelLab work on the account to explain the gap. Section 4d's table was written
in printed costs, so approving against it would have set the props budget about
a third too low: the 5-source pilot is about 95 rather than 68, and all 45
entries about 800 rather than 581.

This adds that caveat at the head of section 4d, and the rule that follows from
it: count a task's own recorded calls for its cap, read get_balance with the
queue empty as the cross-check, and treat a printed-versus-meter gap of about a
third as expected rather than a defect.
