Human review result: rejected

Task: NSC-005

Observed problem:
The insufficient-mana feedback changes the color of the mana fill Image.

When current mana is zero, PlayerManaUI sets fillAmount to zero. There are
therefore no visible fill pixels to change color, so the denied-cast feedback
is effectively invisible at exactly the point where it is most important.

The feedback only becomes visible after some mana regenerates.

Required correction:
Insufficient-mana feedback must remain clearly visible when CurrentMana == 0.

Do not temporarily increase fillAmount or otherwise visually imply that the
player has mana they do not actually have.

Prefer feedback on an always-visible portion of the mana indicator, such as
the bar background/frame, or another clearly readable pulse/flash treatment.

After the feedback completes, the UI must return to its normal scene-authored
appearance.

Regression requirement:
Add automated coverage for a denied spend while mana is exactly zero and prove
that the feedback mechanism remains visible/active independent of fillAmount.

Human validation will be repeated after the repaired candidate is produced.
