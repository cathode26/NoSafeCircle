Vincent's design decisions for this GER v3 cycle (2026-09-14). They are recorded in the GDD blockout section 13 "Room size authority", NSC-069 AC-006, NSC-049 AC-001/AC-003 and Docs/Art/Environment/DUNGEON_ART_DIRECTION.md.

1. Room size is not a hard size. Each room may grow up to three times its current width and depth (Ruined Entry 20 x 18 may grow to at most 60 x 54 world units). Door openings, route and lane widths, aisles, circulation clearances and staging areas stay minimums. Proportions, obstacle and furniture footprints, and placement are yours to propose.
2. The camera follows the wizard, so a room may be bigger than one screen. Use several gameplay-camera review shots.
3. Seriously evaluate a larger layout against the room's tactical purpose and the Diablo and Ultima Online references (R01-R17). Choose the size on design merit; do not keep the baseline only because resizing needs follow-up work in other tasks. Record that follow-up as integration obligations.
4. Room catalog: this room task may edit its own room's RoomBounds entry and exit-door center in RoomSceneCatalog.cs and regenerate RoomSceneCatalog.asset. Tasks take turns on those shared files; NSC-049 reconciles shared boundaries and door alignment.
5. Walls: each room uses an Isometric Tilemap for its walls, owned by this room task; tiles may differ per room. Near (south and east) walls are a low cutaway stub about 0.5 units high, tunable after review; far walls stay full height; door frames stay readable; gameplay colliders stay 2.5 units high.
6. Player start: NSC-044 records the Ruined Entry start point (south end, per the GDD START) in RuinedEntryLayout.cs; NSC-049 places the Player and PlayerSpawn there.
7. Think about cover and positioning for a wizard: breaking line of sight from ranged enemies, room to charge Fireball, lanes for Frost Field, space for Force Wave, circling and escape routes, and holding a door for five seconds. Cover blocks shots but does not make enemies forget the wizard.
8. Keep each room's tactical purpose, one continuous floor with doors D1 to D5 in order, and D5 as the final door.
