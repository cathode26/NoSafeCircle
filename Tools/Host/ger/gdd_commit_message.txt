GDD: make five-room blockout sizes a maximum envelope for GER

Vincent decided on 2026-09-14 that the approved five-room blockout sizes are
baselines, not fixed sizes. Each room may grow to three times its listed width
and depth. Room proportions, obstacle footprints and placement become Task
Design GER proposals, guided by the dungeon art direction and the 17 reference
images, while sealed-door openings, route and lane widths, aisles, circulation
and staging clearances stay minimums. One continuous floor, the D1-D5 order and
the final-door identity still apply; accepted room revisions are recorded in the
owning room task contracts and reconciled through NSC-049.

The GDDRAG knowledge base is rebuilt from the revised GDD: 78 chunks with the
same chunk IDs, text changed only in nsc-gdd-016, nsc-gdd-022 and nsc-gdd-051.
gddctl validate and the GDDRAG smoke, integrity and retrieval regression tests
pass.
