Vincent's design direction for this GER cycle (2026-09-14), recorded in the GDD blockout section 13 "Room size authority":

1. Room size is not a hard size. Each room may grow up to three times its current width and three times its current depth (Ruined Entry's 20 x 18 baseline may grow to at most 60 x 54 world units). Choose the size that best serves the room; do not grow it without a reason.
2. Door opening widths, route and lane widths, aisle widths, circulation clearances and staging areas stay minimums. Room proportions, obstacle and furniture footprints, and their placement are yours to propose.
3. Use the Diablo and Ultima Online reference images we supplied (Docs/Art/Environment/References R01-R17 and its README) and the dungeon art direction to shape the room's visuals and composition.
4. Think about cover and positioning for a wizard: breaking line of sight from ranged enemies, room to charge Fireball, lanes for Frost Field, space for Force Wave, circling and escape routes, and holding a door for five seconds. Cover blocks shots but does not make enemies forget the wizard.
5. Keep each room's tactical purpose, one continuous floor with doors D1 to D5 in order, and D5 as the final door. Room-size and door-center changes must be reconciled through NSC-049.
